Questions hotels ask

Partner FAQ

Will this affect our published rates or OTA rate parity?

Your room is never listed on its own. Guests book a bundled celebration package, and no standalone discounted room rate for your hotel is published.

What does it cost us?

Nothing upfront: no listing, setup or monthly fee. You pay a commission only on completed stays. Founding partners get our lowest rate, which we'll share on our first call.

Who are the guests?

Couples celebrating anniversaries, birthdays and date nights. Both guests must be 18 or older with a valid government photo ID, and we share their details with you before arrival.

Can we pause or change dates?

Yes. You control which nights are open. Tell us to block or release dates at any time and we'll update availability straight away.

Do guests book just the room?

Never. Every booking includes the room plus the full package: cake and décor for anniversaries and birthdays, décor for date nights, and dinner for two.

Is there a long-term contract?

No lock-in. We agree the package and policy in writing, and either side can step away with reasonable notice.
