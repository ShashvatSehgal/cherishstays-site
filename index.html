FAQ

Good to know

Who can book?

Any couple where both guests are 18 or older and carry a valid government photo ID. Each partner hotel's check-in policy is confirmed with us in advance.

Why are the prices lower than booking directly?

Our partner hotels offer special rates on their quieter nights. You get a premium stay for less, and the hotel fills a room that would otherwise sit empty.

Can I customise the package?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind (a particular cake, décor theme or dinner preference) and we'll arrange it with the hotel where possible.

How do I pay?

We'll share the booking details and payment options once your date and package are confirmed.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your details are shared only with the hotel you book, and only for check-in.
